4. Methodology & Limitations

Each subdomain was resolved and checked against the scope guard (authorization reference, scope domains, private-address protection, per-host rate limit). WAPTrix then issued read-only GET requests over HTTPS and HTTP, followed redirects only while they remained on the audited host, performed TLS handshakes (observation, trust validation and a capped TLS 1.1 ClientHello to detect deprecated protocol support) and evaluated Burp-style passive rules against the captured responses.

Findings are mapped to CWE, OWASP WSTG v4.2, OWASP Top 10 (2025) and CVSS v3.1 base scores. Per-subdomain scores start at 100 and deduct weighted penalties per unique rule (critical 30, high 15, medium 8, low 3, informational 0, with damped penalties for repeated instances). Grades: A ≥ 90, B ≥ 80, C ≥ 70, D ≥ 60, F < 60.
